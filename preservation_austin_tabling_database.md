## Legend

- **Tabling status:** OPEN = confirmed open to nonprofit/community tables · VEND = vendor-only (fee) · NPO = nonprofit-only · PART = invitation or partner-only · CLOSED = closed or sold out · UNK = unknown · LISTEN = public meeting, tabling not allowed
- **Date confidence:** C = confirmed by an official 2026/2027 source · P = pattern-based (computed; weekday checked) · U = unconfirmed
- **Council districts** are inferred from addresses and marked "(inf.)". Verify with the City's district lookup (austintexas.gov/GIS/CouncilDistrictMap) before relying on them.
- Sources are named by domain in each row; full URLs are in the Sources section at the end.
